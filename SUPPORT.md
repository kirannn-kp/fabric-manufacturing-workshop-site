# Workshop support

Check [troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md) first. During the workshop, send the facilitator a private Teams message with the lab step, product name, time and time zone, exact error text after redaction, and a redacted screenshot if helpful. Tell them if another teammate can continue.

The organizer supplies the workshop Teams channel, the facilitator contact, and the tenant access contact in the private event invitation. Do not post error evidence or tenant details publicly.
